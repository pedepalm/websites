ACME Internal HR Service Desk Topics and Guidance

Overview: ACME Corporation has a service desk where agents take calls for typical HR related topics. The following is a list of topics and their descriptions.

  • Medical
    • Use this for anything Medical related that is not dental or behavioral
    • Topic Matchings
      • Medical issues: Hypertension, diabetes, obesity, respiratory infections, back pain, arthritis, anxiety/depression, allergies, high cholesterol, heart disease, Family medicine physician, internist, pediatrician, cardiologist, dermatologist, OB-GYN, orthopedist, psychiatrist, endocrinologist, gastroenterologist
    • Guidance
      • details on medical benefits can be found at the following URL: https://pedepalm.netlify.app/svcdesk/guidance/medical.html
      • Copay: $30 for a primary care visit, $60 for a specialist visit, and $250 for an emergency room visit. An in-network preventive annual exam has a $0 copay.
      • Individual maximum: The in-network out-of-pocket maximum is $3,000 per plan year.
      • Family maximum: The in-network family out-of-pocket maximum is $6,000 per plan year. After the family maximum is met, covered in-network medical services for every covered family member are paid at 100% for the rest of the plan year.
  • Dental
    • Topic Matchings
      • Routine cleaning/checkup, cavity/tooth decay, toothache, teeth whitening, root canal, tooth extraction, gum disease, broken/chipped tooth, orthodontic treatment, dental x-rays
    • Guidance
      • details on dental benefits can be found at the following URL: https://pedepalm.netlify.app/svcdesk/guidance/dental.html
      • Copay: $0 for an in-network routine cleaning or checkup, limited to two visits per plan year. A basic filling is a $25 copay. Major services such as a root canal, crown, or extraction are covered at 50% after a $50 copay.
      • Individual maximum: The annual dental benefit maximum is $1,500 per covered person.
      • Family maximum: The combined family dental benefit maximum is $4,500 per plan year. After that maximum is met, further dental services are the member's responsibility until the next plan year.
  • Behavioral
    • Topic Matchings
      • Counseling, Psychiatrist / Psychiatry, Therapy/counseling, psychiatric care, prescription medications, substance use treatment, inpatient psychiatric hospitalization, intensive outpatient/partial hospitalization programs, behavioral therapy for kids, crisis intervention services, psychological testing/evaluations, employee assistance program services
    • Guidance
      • details on behavioral benefits can be found at the following URL: https://pedepalm.netlify.app/svcdesk/guidance/behavioral.html
      • Copay: $25 for an in-network therapy or counseling visit and $50 for a psychiatrist visit. Employee assistance program sessions have a $0 copay for the first six visits per plan year.
      • Individual maximum: Outpatient behavioral visits are covered up to 30 visits per covered person per plan year. The in-network out-of-pocket maximum for behavioral care is $2,000 per person.
      • Family maximum: The family out-of-pocket maximum for behavioral care is $4,000 per plan year. After the family maximum is met, covered in-network behavioral services for every covered family member are paid at 100% for the rest of the plan year.
  • Compensation
    • Topic Matchings
      • W2, W4, Commission Check, Paycheck, Bonus, Employee Stock Purchase Plan, ESPP, Salary negotiation, pay raises, bonus structure, promotions, pay equity concerns, benefits enrollment, stock options/equity, overtime pay, severance packages, payroll errors
    • Guidance
      • details on compensation benefits can be found at the following URL: https://pedepalm.netlify.app/svcdesk/guidance/compensation.html
      • W2: Form W-2 is posted in the payroll portal by January 31. If wages or withheld taxes are wrong, Payroll issues a Form W-2c after the correction is confirmed. Confirm the form is available. Do not change amounts on the call.
      • W4: Employees update federal and state withholding in the payroll portal. A Form W-4 received by 5 p.m. Central on the Tuesday before payday applies to that paycheck.
      • Commission Check: Commissions are paid on the second paycheck of the month after the sales period closes and Finance approves the booking. The caller has 15 calendar days from the statement to open a dispute.
      • Paycheck: Pay is biweekly by direct deposit, and payday is Friday. The pay stub is available in the payroll portal on the Wednesday before payday.
      • Bonus: The annual bonus is paid in March to employees who are active on the payout date. An employee on a written performance plan is not eligible for that cycle.
      • bonus structure: The target is a percentage of base pay, set at hire or at the last promotion. Payout is the target multiplied by the company result and the individual rating.
      • Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP): An eligible employee may contribute 1% to 15% of base pay. Shares are purchased at a 15% discount. Offering periods start January 1 and July 1. Enrollment closes 10 days before the period starts.
      • Salary negotiation: New-hire pay is negotiated with the recruiter before the offer is accepted. After the start date, a pay change goes through the annual cycle or an approved promotion.
      • pay raises: Merit increases are effective on the first paycheck in April. Managers enter ratings in February. An off-cycle raise requires Compensation approval.
      • promotions: The new rate is effective on the first of the month after approval. Title and pay band update in the HR system that same day.
      • pay equity concerns: Record the concern, mark the case confidential, and route it to Compensation. Do not promise an adjustment, and do not discuss a coworker's pay.
      • benefits enrollment: A new hire has 31 days from the start date. Annual enrollment is the first two weeks of November and takes effect on January 1. A qualifying life event must be submitted within 31 days of the event.
      • stock options/equity: New-hire and annual grants vest over four years, with 25% at the one-year mark and monthly after that. Send questions to Equity Administration. Do not quote a grant value on the call.
      • overtime pay: A nonexempt employee earns one and a half times the regular rate for hours worked over 40 in the workweek. The manager approves overtime in the time system before the pay period closes.
      • severance packages: Severance is only what a written Employee Relations agreement states. Do not quote weeks of pay, benefit continuation, or a signature deadline. Route the case to Employee Relations.
      • payroll errors: Report a missing or incorrect payment within two pay periods. A confirmed net underpayment of $500 or more can be paid off-cycle within three business days. A smaller amount is added to the next paycheck.
  • Discrimination
    • Topic Matchings
      • Passed over on promotion, Color of my skin, racism, nationality, politics, religion, religious beliefs, Race/ethnicity discrimination, gender discrimination, age discrimination, disability discrimination, religious discrimination, sexual orientation discrimination, pregnancy discrimination, national origin discrimination, retaliation for reporting, harassment based on protected class
    • Guidance
      • details on discrimination benefits can be found at the following URL: https://pedepalm.netlify.app/svcdesk/guidance/discrimination.html
      • Passed over on promotion: Record the job, the decision date, and who selected the other candidate. Mark the case confidential and route it to Employee Relations the same day. Do not share the other person's rating or pay.
      • Color of my skin: Treat this as a race concern. Capture the caller's words, the date, the location, and the names involved. Route to Employee Relations. Do not ask the caller to describe their appearance.
      • racism: Record what was said or done, who was present, and when it happened. Route to Employee Relations as Severity 1. Do not debate whether the conduct was racism.
      • nationality: Record the country or national origin the caller named and the action they say was based on it. Route to Employee Relations.
      • politics: Record the political view named and the employment action. Mark the case confidential and route it to Employee Relations. Do not tell the caller whether the concern is illegal.
      • religion: Record the religion named, the practice involved (schedule, dress, or prayer), and the manager's response. Route to Employee Relations.
      • religious beliefs: Record the belief, the accommodation the caller asked for, and whether it was refused. Route to Employee Relations the same day.
      • Race/ethnicity discrimination: Record the race or ethnicity named, the employment action, and the dates. Route to Employee Relations the same day.
      • gender discrimination: Record the action, such as a job, pay, schedule, or comment, and who made the decision. Route to Employee Relations.
      • age discrimination: Record any age the caller volunteers, the age-related comment or decision, and the job. Route to Employee Relations. Do not ask for a birth date on the call.
      • disability discrimination: Record a condition only if the caller volunteers it, plus the accommodation requested and the response. Route to Employee Relations. Do not request medical records.
      • religious discrimination: Record the religion, the adverse action, and the dates. Route to Employee Relations the same day.
      • sexual orientation discrimination: Record what was said or decided and who was involved. Route to Employee Relations. Do not ask the caller to describe their orientation beyond what they already stated.
      • pregnancy discrimination: Record the action, such as leave, schedule, job removal, or comments, and the dates. Route to Employee Relations the same day.
      • national origin discrimination: Record the national origin named and the employment action. Route to Employee Relations the same day.
      • retaliation for reporting: Record the earlier report, the date it was made, and the action the caller says followed it. Route to Employee Relations as Severity 1 and flag the case as retaliation.
      • harassment based on protected class: Record the protected class named, the conduct, how often it happens, and whether it is still happening. If the caller describes an immediate threat to safety, also use the Emergency topic. Otherwise route to Employee Relations the same day.
  • Emergency
    • Topic Matchings
      • Anything that sounds like an emergency
    • Guidance
      • details on emergencies can be found at the following URL: https://pedepalm.netlify.app/svcdesk/guidance/emergency.html
  • Fraud
    • Topic Matchings
      • Identity theft, taking money under the table, improper signing contracts, insider trading
    • Guidance
      • details on fraud benefits can be found at the following URL: https://pedepalm.netlify.app/svcdesk/guidance/fraud.html
      • Identity theft: Record whose identity was used, which accounts or payroll records were involved, and when the caller noticed it. Mark the case confidential and route it to Corporate Security the same day. If personal bank accounts are involved, tell the caller to contact the bank. Do not reset passwords or change direct deposit on the call.
      • taking money under the table: Record who received the payment, the amount if the caller knows it, the dates, and whether it was cash or a personal transfer. Mark the case confidential and route it to Internal Audit as Severity 1. Do not contact the person named.
      • improper signing contracts: Record the contract, who signed it, whether that person had authority, and the date. Route the case to Legal and Internal Audit the same day. Do not tell the caller to cancel or re-sign the contract.
      • insider trading: Record the security, the person named, what nonpublic information was used, and the trade date if the caller knows it. Mark the case confidential and route it to Legal and Corporate Security immediately. Do not tell the caller to buy, sell, or share the information.

Topic List and their Default Severity Table

The following lists the Valid Topics followed by the default severity level of each of the above issues. This is to be used when creating a case, however, the caller can change the severity by request. Use the exact syntax for the topic.

  • Medical = Severity 3
  • Dental = Severity 3
  • Behavioral = Severity 3
  • Compensation = Severity 3
  • Discrimination = Severity 1
  • Emergency = Severity 1
  • Fraud = Severity 1
