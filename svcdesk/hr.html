ACME Internal HR Service Desk Topics

Overview: ACME Corporation has a service desk where agents take calls for typical HR related topics. The following is a list of topics and their descriptions.

Medical

  • Use this for anything Medical related that is not dental or behavioral
  • Valid topics
    • Medical issues: Hypertension, diabetes, obesity, respiratory infections, back pain, arthritis, anxiety/depression, allergies, high cholesterol, heart disease, Family medicine physician, internist, pediatrician, cardiologist, dermatologist, OB-GYN, orthopedist, psychiatrist, endocrinologist, gastroenterologist
  • Guidance – no troubleshooting guidance

Dental

  • Valid topics
    • Routine cleaning/checkup, cavity/tooth decay, toothache, teeth whitening, root canal, tooth extraction, gum disease, broken/chipped tooth, orthodontic treatment, dental x-rays
  • Guidance – no troubleshooting guidance

Behavioral

  • Valid topics
    • Counseling, Psychiatrist / Psychiatry, Therapy/counseling, psychiatric care, prescription medications, substance use treatment, inpatient psychiatric hospitalization, intensive outpatient/partial hospitalization programs, behavioral therapy for kids, crisis intervention services, psychological testing/evaluations, employee assistance program services

Compensation

  • Valid topics
    • W2, W4, Commission Check, Paycheck, Bonus, Employee Stock Purchase Plan, ESPP, Salary negotiation, pay raises, bonus structure, promotions, pay equity concerns, benefits enrollment, stock options/equity, overtime pay, severance packages, payroll errors
  • Guidance – no troubleshooting guidance

Discrimination

  • Valid topics
    • Passed over on promotion, Color of my skin, racism, nationality, politics, religion, religious beliefs, Race/ethnicity discrimination, gender discrimination, age discrimination, disability discrimination, religious discrimination, sexual orientation discrimination, pregnancy discrimination, national origin discrimination, retaliation for reporting, harassment based on protected class
  • Guidance – no troubleshooting guidance

Emergency

  • Valid topics
    • Anything that sounds like an emergency
  • Guidance – no troubleshooting guidance

Fraud

  • Valid topics
    • Identity theft, taking money under the table, improper signing contracts, insider trading
  • Guidance – no troubleshooting guidance

Topic List and their Default Severity

The following lists the valid topics followed by the default severity level of each of the above issues. This is to be used when creating a case, however, the caller can change the severity by request. Use the exact syntax for the topic.

MedicalSeverity 3
