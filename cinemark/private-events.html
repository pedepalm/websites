FAQ

What are Private Events at Cinemark?

Private Events give groups the opportunity to gather in a movie theater setting for celebrations, screenings, meetings, independent film screenings, gaming experiences, and other special occasions.

What types of events can be hosted at Cinemark?

Cinemark offers event options for private movie screenings, business gatherings, field trips, church services, birthday parties, gaming experiences, and other group events.

Why host an event at a movie theater?

Movie theaters offer large screens, immersive sound, comfortable seating, and a unique shared experience that can help make events more memorable.

Who can book a Private Event?

Private Events are designed for businesses, schools, community organizations, churches, families, and groups looking for a unique venue experience.

How do I get started with a Private Event?

Guests can submit an inquiry through the Private Events page to explore available event options and find the right experience for their group.

What makes Cinemark Private Events unique?

Cinemark combines the excitement of the big screen with flexible event experiences designed to bring people together.
